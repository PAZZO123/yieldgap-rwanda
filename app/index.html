      How to read this

      • Benchmark = the yield the top quarter of districts already reach for this crop and season (75th percentile). It is a level other Rwandan farmers achieved in 2025, not a research-station maximum.
      • Reliable estimate = at least 200 ha of the crop harvested in the district. Districts with less are shown in grey and left out of the benchmark and the totals.
      • Extra harvest = gap (kg/ha) × the district's harvested area. Tonnes are not added across crops: fresh cassava and dry beans don't compare by weight.
      • What-if uses only practices with a strong, positive link to the crop's yield that also holds between districts in the same province. It uses the smaller of the two estimates, lets a practice rise only to the highest level any district already reached, and never goes past the best district's yield. These are associations between districts, not guaranteed effects: soils, rainfall and markets differ too.
