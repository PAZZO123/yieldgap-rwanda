## Longer sentences (read these in the app too)

Open the app with `?lang=rw` and read: the highlight bar (the four findings), the "In words" paragraph of a district, the guide steps and the "How it works" notes. They are built from the sentence templates in `app/i18n.js` (keys starting with `hl`, `brief`, `peer`, `tour`, `note`).
