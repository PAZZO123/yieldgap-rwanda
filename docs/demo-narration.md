# Demo video: narration script

A 2-minute screen recording of the live app (<https://pazzo123.github.io/yieldgap-rwanda/>) with a
narration track. Captions are in [demo-captions.srt](demo/demo-captions.srt).

**Narration voice: synthetic.** The voice is Microsoft's neural voice `en-KE-AsiliaNeural` (Kenyan
English, female), generated with the `edge-tts` tool. The script was drafted with AI help and is
based on figures that were checked against the data (see [AI_USE.md](AI_USE.md)). The screen
recording was scripted and captured automatically from the live site; the position shown for
"Find my district" is a demo position (Musanze).

| Time | Narration |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome. This is the Rwanda Yield-Gap Explorer, built by Team The Pillars for the NISR Big Data Hackathon, Track 1: Agricultural Productivity. |
| 0:11 | Rwanda aims to raise crop productivity by fifty percent, yet the same crop yields very differently from one district to the next. We ask three questions: where do harvests fall behind, what is closing the gap worth, and what helps? Our data: NISR's twenty twenty-five Seasonal Agricultural Survey. |
| 0:31 | These key insights are computed live. Closing every cassava gap in Season B, for example, would add about thirteen point four billion francs of value, more than any other crop. Tap Show me, and the app takes you to the evidence. |
| 0:47 | Pick a season and a crop, here maize in Season A. The benchmark is what the top quarter of districts already reach: one thousand nine hundred and thirty kilograms per hectare. Closing the gap would add about thirty-two thousand tonnes. |
| 1:01 | The map shades each district by its gap to the benchmark; darker means further behind. Zoom, drag, and switch the view to yield, extra harvest or value in francs. Tap a district for its numbers. |
| 1:15 | On a phone, the pin button finds the district you are standing in and opens its statistics. Your position stays on your device. For this demo, we set it to Musanze. |
| 1:28 | The crop priorities table ranks every crop by the value its gap is worth. In Bugesera, bush beans beat maize, because a kilogram of beans is worth almost twice as much. |
| 1:40 | Every district gets a plain-language summary. Bugesera harvested sixteen hundred kilograms of maize per hectare, sixteen and a half percent below the benchmark; closing the gap would add three thousand tonnes. |
| 1:53 | What-if sliders raise a practice, like improved seed, up to the best level any district has reached, and show the likely gain. We only use links that are statistically strong and hold within provinces, and we apply the cautious estimate. |
| 2:08 | Similar districts come from a nearest-neighbour model, tested by leaving each district out. Ngoma, a similar district, yields twenty percent more than Bugesera. The PSTA 5 tracker shows the distance to the national seed and fertilizer targets. |
| 2:25 | We tried to be honest: errors are clustered by district, the model's real accuracy gain is seven and a half percent, and every figure in our report is checked against the data. |
| 2:35 | It works in English and Kinyarwanda, in light or dark mode. A guide helps new users. You can download the data, print a one-page district sheet, or share a link to this exact view. |
| 2:49 | The explorer is live, free, and open source. Thank you. |
